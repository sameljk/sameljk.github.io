## Private tutoring

I offer one-to-one maths tutoring for GCSE, A-Level (all exam boards) and first- and second-year undergraduate study . Sessions run online, typically weekly for 1-2 hours, and I tailor them around exam boards, or just building confidence with a specific topic.
